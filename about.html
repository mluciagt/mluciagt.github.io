About

I immigrated to Quebec and ended up in the boreal forest, among lakes and trees, with an enduring curiosity for nature and a passion for understanding life’s remarkable diversity, from hidden microbial worlds to landscape dynamics, and how we interact with it. I’m currently a Biology M.Sc. student in Sherbrooke, weaving together microbiology, ecology, and bioinformatics to characterize fungal communities associated with mosses across different ecosystems. I also capture life’s diversity through visual records, photographing events, candid moments, and my travels. Through photography, I love exploring new places, meeting people, and finding beauty in the everyday.
